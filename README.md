# Netflix Content Analysis

Exploratory data analysis of Netflix's catalog of 8,800+ titles using Python.

## Objective
Analyze Netflix content trends by type, country, genre, rating, and release year to uncover patterns in their catalog.

## Tools & Libraries
- Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn

## Key Findings
- Netflix has 2x more Movies than TV Shows
- The United States produces the most content, followed by India and the UK
- International Movies and Dramas are the most common genres
- TV-MA is the most frequent rating — Netflix skews toward adult content
- Content additions peaked in 2019 before declining
